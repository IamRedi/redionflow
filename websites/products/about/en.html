Processes

Every process, in the crew’s pocket.

The office works on the computer, the crew on the phone. This is how the main processes look, with sample data.

Live Workzone

Today’s sites with crew, progress, notes and equipment. One tap opens the site.

Tree atlas

Every tree has its own card. The atlas shows the species illustrated: shape, leaf, bark, flower.

Fleet & inspections

Every vehicle with plate, photo, location and status: out, in the workshop or free. Inspection dates are not forgotten.

Workshop & repairs

A defect is reported with a photo or a scan. The workshop accepts, repairs and releases it, with history.

Wood workshop

From incoming timber to packed firewood: intake, production and stock, with quantities and orders.

Inventory

Saws, machines and tools with a code. One scan shows where it is, who has it and whether it is fine.

Dashboard view

Hours of the month, progress per site and the crew in clear charts. Also on the big office screen.

AI assistant

Ask in your own language. The answer comes from the company’s rules and manuals, with a source.

Free tools

Free tools for companies.

Alongside the platform we offer tools that help crews every day. The first are KiDraht and ArborTwin.

KiDraht

Free · live

The knowledge library for field work: illustrated books, basic courses and tests on tree care, construction and safety. AI translation into the worker’s language and an assistant that answers from the company’s rules.

Open the library

ArborTwin

In development · local beta

The digital twin of a tree. From photo to documentation: AI helps with measurements, the specialist reviews them, wind data comes from DWD and the report is a PDF. Opens from the tree card in BaumdienstFlow and as a tool in KiDraht.

  1. Photo
  2. AI
  3. Review
  4. Report

A documentation aid. It does not replace a safety inspection by a specialist.
